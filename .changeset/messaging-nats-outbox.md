Implement the NATS JetStream messaging of the kit, in two entry points, aligned with the users, subjects and streams of cadenway-ops.

- `@cadenway/nest-messaging`, usable without a database: `MessagingModule.forRoot`/`forRootAsync` (one connection that waits for its first connect, reconnects forever, uses the inbox prefix `_INBOX.<user>` and drains on shutdown; `manageStreams` off by default, in which case the streams the service uses are checked at start-up), `messagingConfigShape` (`NATS_URL`, `NATS_USER`, `NATS_PASSWORD`), `EventPublisher` (subject derived from the event name, `Nats-Msg-Id`, `traceparent`, organization and event-name headers, validation with the schemas of `@cadenway/contracts`), restricted events (`restrictedEvents`: published on `restricted.<name>` in `EVENTS_RESTRICTED`), `EventConsumers.subscribe` (one durable pull consumer `<service>__<handler>` per event with a single filter subject, `AckWait` 30 s, five deliveries, retries after 1 s, 5 s, 30 s and 2 min, invalid payloads terminated, organization context and consumer span; `manageConsumers` on by default; consumers are never deleted nor listed), the optional `EventDeduplicator`, `DeadLetterWorker` (off by default, run by notification-service: advisories of `EVENTS` and `EVENTS_RESTRICTED`, copies on `dlq.<consumer>.<subject>`), `RebuildEmitter` and `EventConsumers.onRebuild` on `rebuild.<consumer user>.<entity>` (plain publish by default, request and reply as an option), `messagingHealthIndicator`.
- `@cadenway/nest-messaging/outbox`, with `drizzle-orm` and `pg`: the tables `outboxEvents`, `processedEvents` and `eventVersions`, `OutboxWriter.append` in the use case transaction, `OutboxRelay` (`for update skip locked`, several replicas, `wake()`, periodic purge), `purgeSentOutboxEvents`, `purgeProcessedEvents`, `requeueOutboxEvents`, the PostgreSQL deduplicator with `claimVersion`, and `OutboxModule.forRoot`.

Migration for every service that imports `/outbox`: re-export `outboxEvents`, `processedEvents` and `eventVersions` from a `src/modules/*/infrastructure/persistence/*.schema.ts` file, run `drizzle-kit generate`, and add `ALTER TABLE "outbox_events" FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;` to the generated migration. A service that imports only the main entry point has nothing to migrate.
